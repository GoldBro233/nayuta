Some pages only need words, a few links, and room to breathe.

This example lives in `src/content/pages/demo/markdown.md`. It has no local sidebar components, so the left side uses `src/content/_left.astro`. With no root or local right component, the reading area has no right sidebar.

## A small reading list

- A notebook for unfinished ideas.
- A novel with sentences worth reading twice.
- A blank page for whatever arrives next.

> Leave enough space around an idea for the reader to meet it halfway.
